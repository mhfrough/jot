Jot / Changelog

Changelog

1.2.1 24 October 2026

  • The toast sizes to its text instead of wrapping into a narrow column.
  • On small phones (360 px, like the Realme C25s) every editor tool fits on one row.
  • The install sheet shows screenshots, a description and the new-note shortcut.

1.2.0 24 October 2026

  • Formatting now shows as formatting: bold is bold, with no marks on screen. Notes are still stored as plain text.
  • Switching tabs switches the note on the right, and closing Locked closes an open locked note.
  • Accessibility and SEO pass: stronger contrast for labels and dates, a main heading and landmark in the app, structured data for the app and the questions.
  • Real platform logos on the Get Jot section. Apps for Windows, Linux, Android, macOS and iOS are coming soon.

1.1.0 24 October 2026

  • Locked: a tab with its own password. Its notes are encrypted on the device (AES-256-GCM, PBKDF2 with 600,000 rounds), close after a few idle minutes or 30 seconds in the background, and stay encrypted in exports.
  • Formatting: bold, italic, strike and code, shown as you type, with Ctrl B, Ctrl I, Ctrl Shift X and Ctrl E.
  • Ticked checklist lines are struck through and faded.
  • Notes are mono only, so formatting always lines up with the text.

1.0.1 24 October 2026

  • Square corners everywhere in the app.
  • The icon's cursor is ochre, and the Android icon keeps it inside the safe circle.
  • A new version now says so with a Reload button, instead of waiting quietly.
  • A Get Jot section for the web, Android, iPhone and iPad, Windows, macOS and Linux.
  • New pages: Design, Changelog and a 404 page.

1.0.0 24 October 2026

  • Plain notes with no sign-in, kept in the browser on your device.
  • Created and updated dates on every note, pins, search, checklists, archive and trash with undo.
  • Paper and carbon themes, JetBrains Mono, an ochre signal.
  • Export to JSON; import from Simple Notes, Fossify Notes and text files.
